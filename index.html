Glide
A / ← left kick   D / → right kick
LT / RT on a controller do the same.
S / ↓ or stick down: snow plow. W / ↑ or stick up: double pole (tap again to pole again). Kicking ends either.
Hold each kick to push it all the way out (a quick tap only gives part of the power) and to ride the glide, and let go before you kick again. Alternate to go straight. Each kick turns you one step: left kicks step right, right kicks step left. Repeating a leg makes the step grow; alternate and the steps cancel.
