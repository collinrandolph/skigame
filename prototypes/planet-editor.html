Planet

Trails

A carved trail takes over the ground beside it out to the corridor's width past its bed: nothing there is steeper than the setting (it cuts and fills as little as it can), so the ground beside every trail skis, and trails side by side, at junctions and at forks share one smooth surface. Past the corridor it eases back into the land over 10 m. Joined trails (alongside within the join distance, or a trail going back over its own path) ski and carve as one. Once Terrain cleanup is applied it shapes the ground beside the trails instead, and these two settings do nothing.

Clean up

Trails running side by side within the join distance always ski and carve as one. Clean up also thins crowded points, joins points of different trails that nearly meet, snaps loose ends onto the trail they stop short of, and eases small patches too steep to ski (not painted cliffs). The game does the same as it loads, without changing the layout; here it changes the layout (Ctrl+Z undoes it).

Terrain cleanup

Analyse reads the land (as sculpted, before the trails carve it) over the whole planet: cliffs and mountains to keep, steep mistakes to smooth away, pockets out of reach, narrow ridges, valleys and benches, cliff feet and tops, and where the trails cross steep ground. Plan turns that into the target map: which ground should end up skiable (trail beds and aprons, benches cut back, slots through ridges, mistakes and thin strips smoothed away) and which unskiable (solid masses, narrow gaps closed, tall walls kept). Neither changes anything. Apply shapes the ground to the plan (planning first if needed): trail beds held level with gentle shoulders, skiable ground smoothed as little as it can be to under the limit, mountains' feet eased back beside a cut, gaps raised into the walls. The change is kept as a layer over your sculpting (which stays as it was): Remove takes it off, Ctrl+Z undoes it, and Apply again redoes it from scratch after you edit.
